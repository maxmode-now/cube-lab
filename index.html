Cube Lab

Cube Lab is a free browser trainer for Rubik’s cubes. Play 2×2 through 5×5, learn the beginner method, CFOP, and Roux, scan a physical cube from photos, and follow beginner steps or a fast solve. A WCA-style timer runs in Play. No account required.

Modes

  • Play — scramble, inspect, and time solves on a 3D cube.
  • Learn — beginner, Ortega, CFOP, Roux, and case libraries with demos.
  • Solve — beginner steps or a fast computer search for the current cube.
  • Scan — capture or paint all six faces, then open a guided solve.
