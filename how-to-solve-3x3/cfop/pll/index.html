Before you start

This page is the PLL part of CFOP. Finish OLL first so the whole top face is yellow and white stays on the bottom.

2-Look PLL does not try to solve every last-layer permutation in one algorithm. Instead, you place the corners first, then the edges. That is why it is much easier to learn than full PLL.

Demo plays the case, then the fix. Cube Lab resets, applies the inverse instantly so you see the pattern, then animates the algorithm.

1. Corner positions

Read headlights. Your goal is to seat the four top corners. Edges can wait.

  • A-perm: one pair of headlights. Hold them at the back and cycle the other three corners.
  • E-perm: no headlights. Two diagonal corner pairs swap.

If no corner is correct yet, apply A-perm once and look again. A diagonal swap can also be A-perm twice.

A-perm (a)
R' F R' B2 R F' R' B2 R2
E-perm
R B' R' F R B R' F' R B R' F R B' R' F'

2. Edge positions

Once the corners are seated, only four edge cases remain: Ua, Ub, H, and Z.

  • U-perm: one solved edge. Hold it at the back.
  • H-perm: headlights on all four sides. Opposite edges swap.
  • Z-perm: diagonal bars of matching colour. Adjacent edges swap.

Finish with an AUF (a turn of the top) so the side colours line up.

U-perm (a)
R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2
H-perm
M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2
