How to solve a 2×2 with Ortega

A speed method for the Pocket Cube: one solved layer, seven OLL algorithms, then five PBL algorithms. Same three stages as the beginner guide — but every last-layer case has a fixed recipe.

Before you start

Ortega is the usual 2×2 step after your first solve. If you have not finished a 2×2 yet, use the 2×2 beginner method first — the first layer is identical.

Turn notation: cube notation reference. The OLL and PBL algorithms on this page use outer faces only (U D L R F B) — no wide r / Rw.

Three stages. (1) First layer · (2) OLL — orient the top yellow · (3) PBL — permute both layers at once. Twelve algorithms total for a full speed solve (7 + 5).
